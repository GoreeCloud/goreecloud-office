# Benefits

## Current Foundation Benefits

- Establishes one shared document model before multiple editors diverge.
- Keeps core mutation semantics independent from user-interface frameworks.
- Preserves logical Unicode text while validating mutation boundaries.
- Makes undo and redo deterministic through reversible commands.
- Creates stable identities that can support later persistence, collaboration, and versioning work.
- Uses strict source validation to catch formatting, compilation, test, and lint failures before promotion.

## Planned Product Benefits

- Local-first and offline-capable productivity.
- Portable document ownership and import/export.
- Privacy-focused operation without advertising or behavioral tracking.
- Shared editing infrastructure across Writer, Spreadsheet, Presentations, and Forms.
- Cross-platform native-feeling experiences.
- Recovery, accessibility, interoperability, and self-hosting where server capabilities are required.

Planned benefits are objectives and must not be interpreted as currently delivered end-user functionality.
