# Branding

The product identity is **GoreeCloud Office**.

Installed application labels may use concise product names appropriate to platform conventions while documentation, releases, About/provenance surfaces, and repository records retain the full GoreeCloud product identity.

All future GoreeCloud-controlled visual identity assets and interfaces must follow the applicable current Stable Glaze UI requirements.

The current source foundation contains no approved Office graphical product artwork and makes no visual-conformance claim.
