# Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Office should meet mature productivity expectations without copying proprietary source, confidential behavior, protected assets, or trade dress.

Objectives include:

- Reliable local editing and recovery.
- Strong ODF and OOXML interoperability.
- High-quality text, layout, charts, printing, and PDF export.
- Keyboard-first desktop productivity and touch-first mobile/tablet experiences.
- Platform-native client behavior.
- Accessible, polished current-Stable Glaze UI presentation.
- Self-hostable collaboration without making network services prerequisites for local editing.
- Open, inspectable, recoverable native formats.
- No advertising, sponsored placement, behavioral tracking, or required vendor cloud.

This document records objectives, not current capability claims.
