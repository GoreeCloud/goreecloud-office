# Competitive Objectives

## Benchmark Products

GoreeCloud Office may benchmark mature productivity environments including LibreOffice, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, ONLYOFFICE, and Apple iWork to understand user expectations, interoperability requirements, accessibility needs, reliability patterns, and workflow gaps.

## Objectives Worth Matching

- Dependable word processing, spreadsheet, and presentation workflows.
- Strong open-format and common-format interoperability.
- High-quality printing and export.
- Mature keyboard, accessibility, template, review, and recovery workflows.
- Reliable performance on large working documents.

## Objectives GoreeCloud Intends to Exceed

- Local-first operation without requiring an account or network for core editing.
- Clear ownership of ordinary local files.
- Privacy by default with no advertising, behavioral tracking, or monetized recommendations.
- Self-hostable collaboration and synchronization boundaries where network services are used.
- Explicit separation among creation, storage, managed records, synchronization, and recovery.
- Inspectable native package formats designed for portability and partial recovery.

## Approaches Intentionally Rejected

- Mandatory cloud dependence for basic editing.
- Opaque formats used primarily to lock users into one application.
- Silent content modification by intelligence features.
- Treating synchronization as backup.
- Building each editor around incompatible independent cores.

Competitive objectives guide independent engineering. They do not authorize copying proprietary source code, protected assets, product-specific trade dress, or confidential information.
