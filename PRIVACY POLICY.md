# GoreeCloud Office Privacy Policy — Development Baseline

## Current state

GoreeCloud Office is under development and has no production service or telemetry pipeline in this source slice.

## Product privacy direction

Core local Office editing must not require:

- A GoreeCloud account.
- Network access.
- Remote fonts.
- Remote scripts.
- Remote templates.
- Advertising.
- Behavioral tracking.
- Mandatory external analytics.

Documents and document metadata must not be transmitted merely because a file is opened locally.

Future synchronization, collaboration, intelligence, diagnostics, sharing, and server-backed features require explicit privacy boundaries and applicable Privacy Shield integration before production acceptance.

This development baseline is not a substitute for future deployment-specific privacy disclosures.
